About Me

"I’m Fajle Rabby, a caffeine-fueled explorer on a mission to prove that the world isn’t as scary as the news makes it out to be. After quitting my corporate job to chase horizons, I started this vlog to take you behind the scenes of my most rugged adventures—from sleeping under the stars in the Sahara to navigating the chaotic markets of Ho Chi Minh City. My goal is to provide you with the raw, unfiltered truth about solo travel and the practical tips you need to stop dreaming about your bucket list and start living it."
